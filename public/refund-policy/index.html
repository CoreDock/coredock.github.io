Refund Policy

CoreDock is digital source code delivered electronically. This policy explains when we refund and how to request one.

Important: This policy has not been externally reviewed by legal counsel. Mandatory consumer rights and remedies that cannot lawfully be waived always take priority over this policy.

1. When we refund

We provide a full refund when a verified problem with the purchase cannot reasonably be resolved, including:

  • the repository or delivered files are materially corrupt, incomplete or unusable when the documented setup is followed on a supported environment;
  • you paid but access to the Product was not granted and we cannot restore it;
  • you were charged more than once for the same license;
  • the Product is materially different from a specific claim made on the sales page at the time of purchase; or
  • the charge was not authorized by you.

2. Change of mind

Where applicable law permits us to decline, we do not provide a contractual refund simply because you changed your mind after receiving working source code that matches the description, chose another technology stack, or expected a feature that was never advertised.

This rule does not override a statutory withdrawal right or mandatory consumer remedy that applies to your purchase.

3. Refund timing

A claim that the Product is materially different from the sales description should normally be made within 14 calendar days of purchase. Verified delivery failure, duplicate charges, unauthorized charges and genuine defects are not subject to that contractual 14-day limit.

If applicable law gives you a longer period or an additional remedy, that law prevails.

4. How to request a refund

  1. Email coredock@outlook.com with the subject “Refund request”.
  2. Include your order reference and the email address used for the purchase.
  3. Describe what happened and which refund ground applies.
  4. For a technical defect, include the relevant error, operating system and Docker version when possible.

We aim to provide an initial reply within 2 business days and to decide a complete request within 10 business days. Those targets do not limit mandatory rights.

5. Processing an approved refund

Polar Software, Inc. is the merchant of record for the transaction. Approved refunds are processed through Polar and returned through the original payment method, subject to Polar and payment-network processing times.

After a full refund, the CoreDock license ends and you must stop using and delete copies of the reusable CoreDock foundation, except where applicable law or an already-delivered finished client application creates rights that survive.

6. EU and UK consumers

Consumers in the European Union and United Kingdom may have a 14-day withdrawal right for qualifying distance contracts. Digital-content rules can affect when that right is lost.

CoreDock does not currently rely on a waiver of that withdrawal right for EU or UK consumers. Where applicable law grants the right, it remains available unless and until a compliant consent and acknowledgement flow is actually implemented.

7. Other jurisdictions

Consumer law varies by country. If the law applicable to your purchase gives you rights this policy does not mention, those rights remain in force. Nothing here is intended to exclude a right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded.

8. Abuse and fraud

Where applicable law permits, we may decline abusive or fraudulent refund requests, including repeated purchase-and-refund behavior or continued use of CoreDock after a completed refund.

9. Changes

We may update this policy for future purchases. The version available when you purchase governs that purchase, subject to mandatory law.
